Core Disciplines

What I Do

Reliability Engineering

FMEA / FMECA, RBD modelling, MTBF prediction, Fault Tree Analysis, Weibull analysis, Markov models, and Monte Carlo simulation to quantify risk and guide design decisions.

Safety Assurance

System Safety Assessments, Functional Hazard Assessments, Preliminary System Safety Assessments, and Event Tree Analysis aligned to ARP 4761, DEF-STAN, and IEC standards.

Integrated Logistics Support

Through-life support strategy, maintenance planning, spares optimisation (Poisson modelling), obsolescence management, and ARM case reporting per ASD S2000M and DEF-STAN 00-600.

RAM / RAMS Analysis

Whole-life reliability, availability, maintainability, and supportability modelling for complex systems — from initial concept through in-service lifecycle management.

Probabilistic Risk Analysis

Probability of Sufficiency (POS) sparing, statistical confidence analysis, physics-of-failure modelling, and Arrhenius degradation models for novel materials and environments.

Regulatory Compliance

FDA 21 CFR, EU MDR, EASA CS-27/CS-25, IEC 60601-1, ISO 26262, EN 50126 — bridging engineering evidence with regulatory requirements across multiple international frameworks.
